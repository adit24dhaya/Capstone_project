## Project Fit

Working title:

**Real-Time Embedded PCB Defect Inspection Using High-Resolution YOLO and Deployment-Oriented Detector Analysis**

The project can be framed around applied computer vision for industrial inspection, with emphasis on:

- High-resolution YOLO11 detector comparison for six PCB defect classes.
- Accuracy/efficiency tradeoffs for real-time inspection.
- Defect-size analysis, calibration/reliability, and inspection cost metrics.
- Deployment readiness through ONNX/export and Nautilus/Kubernetes GPU experiments.
